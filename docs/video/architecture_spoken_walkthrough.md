# Architecture Walkthrough - Spoken Draft

If you look at the architecture, everything starts on the left with the data we actually need for the investment use case. We have structured portfolio information such as funds, positions, prices and benchmarks, and we also have unstructured research such as earnings reports, filings, investor updates, transcripts and analyst notes. We bring those sources into the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, where we first prepare and govern the data. The documents are parsed and converted into searchable chunks, while the structured portfolio data is modeled as Delta tables.

From there, Unity Catalog becomes the governance foundation across the project. On the structured side we will add business semantics, Metric Views and Genie Ontology so the platform understands concepts like a current position or company exposure instead of only seeing column names. On the research side, Databricks AI Search gives us semantic and hybrid retrieval over the document corpus. We will also use governed UC Functions for deterministic portfolio logic, Prompt Registry for versioning our prompts, AI Gateway for controlled model access, and we will experiment with ai_decide for structured AI decisions.

Then we build the multi-agent layer. The Research Agent is responsible for reasoning over research documents and returning evidence-backed conclusions. The Portfolio Agent handles structured investment analytics. The Supervisor Agent coordinates the specialists and combines their results when a question crosses both domains. One important design principle in this project is that we are not making everything an agent. Retrieval stays retrieval, deterministic calculations stay deterministic, business analytics can use Genie, and agents are used where reasoning and orchestration add value.

Finally, we take the system beyond a notebook. MLflow gives us tracing, evaluation, human review and production quality monitoring. The solution is served through a consistent Responses API contract and deployed through Databricks Apps. Lakebase gives us persistent conversation state so follow-up questions can use prior context. The final outcome is one governed application that can answer company research questions, portfolio exposure questions, and cross-domain investment questions with citations and observable reasoning.
